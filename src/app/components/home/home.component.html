Our story

Pizzeria started with one simple idea: a pizza tastes best when you had a say in how it was built. What began as a single counter has grown into a kitchen where every base is stretched, sauced, and topped the way our guests actually want it — not just the way a set menu says.

From the first ball of dough to the last slice out of the oven, we keep the process short and the ingredients honest. No shortcuts, no mystery additions — just classics done properly, alongside a build-your-own counter for anyone who'd rather go their own way.

Every pizza on our regular menu started out on that same counter, tweaked and re-tested until it earned a permanent spot. If you don't see what you're after, the ingredients list is yours to raid — that's really what this place is for.

Ingredients

We keep the pantry small on purpose. Fresh vegetables, real cheese, and house-made sauces — if an ingredient can't earn its place on a pizza, it doesn't make it into the kitchen. Everything is prepped the same day it's served.

Our Chefs

Behind every order is a small crew who've spent years learning how dough behaves, how long each topping needs in the oven, and when a pizza is actually ready to come out. They taste as they go and take the same care on your first order as your fiftieth.
